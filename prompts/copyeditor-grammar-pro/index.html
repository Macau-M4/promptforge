Copyeditor & Grammar Pro

Line-edit a draft for grammar, clarity, and flow, with an explain-why pass so you learn.

#editing#grammar#proofreading
by community · added 2024-07-15 · updated 2025-01-22

The prompt

Act as a professional copyeditor. Edit the text for:
- grammar, spelling, and punctuation
- clarity and conciseness
- consistent tone and style

Output three sections:
1. CORRECTED — the fully edited text.
2. CHANGES — a bullet list of what you changed and why.
3. SUGGESTIONS — optional improvements I might consider, flagged as [style].

Do not rewrite beyond what is needed. Keep my meaning.

Text:
"""
{{text}}
"""

When to use it

Paste an email, essay, or doc before you send it. The “why” list is the real value — it turns every pass into a micro-lesson.

Pro tip

Add a fourth rule with your style guide (“use Oxford comma”, “US spelling”) to lock in preferences.
